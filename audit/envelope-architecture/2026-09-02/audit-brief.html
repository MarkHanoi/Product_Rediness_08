PRYZM European Legal-Envelope Architecture

Proposed: source → country adapter → canonical rules → constraint graph → geometry kernel → legal envelope → development potential. Long version with all evidence: ENVELOPE-ARCHITECTURE-AUDIT-FULL.md.

REVISE

The proposal's destination is sound and largely already built. Its two central novel elements — a general constraint-graph layer and a greenfield 24-token vocabulary — are rejected as rivals to shipped and frozen machinery. Exactly two elements survive as genuinely new work. Rewrite the proposal as an increment over the as-built system.

6 / 115

The most product-critical finding. The never-overstate gate walks only 6 of 115 registered jurisdictions. Paris, Denmark, the Netherlands, Valencia, Sevilla and Switzerland contribute zero gate solves — computeParisEnvelope is never walked by any gate. Gate-enumerability is the strongest argument for data over procedure, and should drive the migration order.

What the vocabulary is missing — five dimensions, all proven by shipped scars

  • Constructions — ESBarcelona's block-level implicit solve with a minimum-vs-equality quantifier (Art. 242.2/350.2; the L-581 mitre kernel peaked at 55.4% sound on real rings).
  • Datum — four legal meanings of "height" in one token: rasant-at-façade (L-584), Paris NGF absolute, Estonia EH2000, Porto mean-ground-at-façade.
  • Denominator basis — DK72–85% of plans are non-parcel-scoped; MAX_GFA without valueBasis re-installs a shipped national bug by design.
  • Precedence — rank/overlay/exception structure; ZoningRecord.overlays is currently consumed by nothing.
  • Applicability + honesty — the UNKNOWN lattice and the cited refusal as first-class outputs.

Headline counter-example: PTPorto's moda da cércea — height = the statistical mode of the block's built frontage. No current kind represents it; it is the audit's #1 missing primitive (CONTEXT_AGGREGATE / fabricDerivedHeight) and the named blocker of the founder-signed Porto pack. A new axis was also found: nothing certifies "never understate an obligation" — the honesty gate is one-directional.

The revision — build this instead

  1. Vocabulary as append-only extension of the two frozen seats — never a third seat. Adopt the 14-row cited primitive table; refuse primitives without a met citation.
  2. No general constraint graph. Constructions enter as typed rule kinds through the proven append-only registry; precedence = rank-as-fact + refuse-on-tie (Catala prior art); finish overlay consumption.
  3. Extend the kernel in priority order: difference op → holes → inclined tops (unlocks German Abstandsflächen + the Paris crown) → skeleton stage 2.
  4. The two accepted novel seams: a true-3D solid stage with per-face rule attribution (or "why this height?" stops tracing to the article), and a versioned evaluation cache.
  5. Mint the never-understate gate and check monotonicity per solve, not by assumption.
  6. Close 6/115 → 115/115, Paris first — a pack migrates to declarative data exactly when that makes it gate-walkable.
  7. AS-IS Catastro data (FXCC, floors, 3D) enters only through the existing typed boundary — subtraction, consolidation, fabric-keyed rules. Never a legal ceiling.
