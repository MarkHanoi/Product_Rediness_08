## 1. VERDICT

# **REVISE**

**— with high confidence, and with a precise meaning:** the proposal's *destination* is sound and
largely **already built**; its two central *novel* elements — a general CONSTRAINT GRAPH layer and
a greenfield 24-token constraint vocabulary — are **rejected as rivals** to shipped and frozen
machinery; and exactly **two of its elements survive as genuinely new, defensible work**. The
proposal should be rewritten as an *increment over the as-built system*, per §4 below. Not
APPROVE-WITH-CHANGES, because the central novel layer is removed, not amended. Not REDESIGN,
because the pipeline shape and the one-engine hypothesis (in weak form) are validated — by the
strongest possible evidence: they are the architecture PRYZM already ships.

## 2. WHAT THE HYPOTHESIS GETS RIGHT AND WRONG

**Right (weak form):** everything the measured corpus has met ultimately renders as rings +
extrusions + clip planes + booleans, and country adapters SHOULD only interpret. This is not a
prediction — it is a description of the shipped E4 architecture (10 country adapters, one frozen
canonical model, one declarative evaluator at Barcelona byte-parity, one envelope engine).

**Wrong (strong form), with the corpus as witness:**
1. **Representation is not the bottleneck — sourcing is.** Measured: envelope parameters are
   state-served in 0/21 European countries; DE's corpus problem is acquisition, not OCR. The
   proposal optimizes the cheap half of the problem.
2. **"Determines" overclaims.** Córdoba's occupation cap does not determine a unique polygon (the
   engine draws a labelled choice); Paris determines per-component and refuses the crown where
   data is absent. The honest output type is {determined | labelled-choice | cited-refusal} per
   component — which the as-built already produces and the never-overstate gate depends on.
3. **Five rule dimensions are invisible to the proposed vocabulary** and are, on the repo's own
   scar tissue, the actual work: **constructions** (Barcelona's block-level implicit solve, the
   Art. 342.5 banded ladder), **datum** (four legal meanings of "height" — rasant-at-façade L-584,
   Paris NGF absolute, EE EH2000, Porto mean-ground-at-façade), **denominator basis** (the Danish
   bebyggelsesprocent bug: 72–85% of plans are non-parcel-scoped; MAX_GFA without `valueBasis`
   re-installs a shipped national bug *by design*), **precedence** (rank/overlay/exception
   structure), **applicability + honesty** (UNKNOWN lattice, refusal as first-class output).

## 3. THE STRONGEST COUNTER-EXAMPLES (all rules PRYZM has actually met)

1. **Porto *moda da cércea*** — height = the statistical MODE of the block's built frontage; a
   fabric-derived value no current kind represents (the repo names the needed kind:
   `fabricDerivedHeight` / lane A's `CONTEXT_AGGREGATE`). **The audit's #1 missing primitive**,
   and now the named blocker of the founder-signed Porto pack.
2. **DK denominator basis** — the national-scale mis-multiplication the R2 `valueBasis` seat fixed.
3. **DE Abstandsflächen 0.4×H** — setback as a function of the building's own height. **Not a
   cycle**: closed form = pointwise-min of inclined planes; the feedback form is the monotone
   bisection `solveBlockDerivedDepth` already ships. But it needs *Land*-specific H-measurement
   semantics at the SEMANTIC layer, and inclined-plane KERNEL support (owed, priority 3).
4. **Barcelona Art. 242.2/350.2** — depth as an implicit solve on the BLOCK with a load-bearing
   minimum-vs-EQUALITY quantifier; beneath it, L-581: the mitre construction peaked at 55.4%
   sound on real cadastral rings and misread geometry failure as legal fact — the reason the
   in-house erosion kernel (inward error bias) exists and must not be replaced.
5. **Explicit-geometry zones** (Madrid NZ1 ring, Barcelona clau 18 = 22.5% of private buildable
   land, Paris ECM) — the polygon IS the rule; a vocabulary can only lossily re-derive it. Needs
   `EXPLICIT_GEOMETRY_REF` as a reference tier, not translation.
6. **MIN-obligations (build-to lines, minimum heights)** — break composition monotonicity, can
   empty the feasible set, and expose a **genuinely new axis: nothing certifies "never understate
   an obligation"** — the never-overstate gate is one-directional. (Part IV §T3.)

## 4. THE REVISED ARCHITECTURE (what to build instead)

**Keep the pipeline's boxes as RESPONSIBILITIES; delete them as NEW COMPONENTS.** The revision:

1. **Vocabulary = append-only extension of the two FROZEN seats** (E1a canonical parameters +
   `factVocabulary.ts`), never a third seat (C84 EI-9). Adopt lane A's 14-row primitive table —
   each row citation-backed, each with its layer assignment: `CONTEXT_AGGREGATE`,
   `HEIGHT_PROPORTIONAL_OFFSET`, `IMPLICIT_BLOCK_SOLVE` (with quantifier), `BANDED_LOOKUP` (with
   edge refusal), `EXPLICIT_GEOMETRY_REF`, `valueBasis`, datum family, rank+combinators,
   applicability facts, UNKNOWN lattice, `BUILD_TO_LINE`, `PROJECTION_ALLOWANCE`,
   `LABELLED_CHOICE` output, terrain-slope fact. Refuse speculative primitives without a met
   citation (sky-exposure, shadow, view-corridor were deliberately refused).
2. **No general constraint graph.** Constructions enter as *typed rule kinds* through the proven
   append-only kind registry with the compile-gated exhaustive switch (~low-double-digit kinds
   for Europe). Precedence = **rank {scheme, level} as a fact + refuse-on-tie** (Catala's
   prioritized-default model is the prior art) — and finish the half-built part:
   `ZoningRecord.overlays` is currently consumed by nothing.
3. **Kernel: extend the in-house 2D-first kernel** in this priority order: (1) a difference op
   (oracle-pinned, under-coverage bias), (2) holes in booleans, (3) **piecewise-planar inclined
   tops as a height field** (unlocks Abstandsflächen + Paris couronnement), (4) skeleton stage 2.
   `manifold-3d` stays display/export-only. Every adoptable JS/W ASM alternative was rejected with
   named failure modes (Part III Q2); a new kernel or three-bvh-csg would violate P2.
4. **The two defensible NEW seams** (the only proposal elements accepted as novel): a **true-3D
   solid stage with PER-FACE rule attribution** — without per-face rule ids the evidence chain
   severs and "why this height?" stops tracing to the article — and a **reference-only evaluation
   cache keyed on `ruleSetVersion`**.
5. **Mint the never-understate-an-obligation gate** as the sibling of never-overstate, and gate
   the monotonicity premise per solve (it is an empirical property of curated content, not a
   theorem — L-581-MONOTONICITY).
6. **Close the gate-coverage hole — the single most product-critical finding:** the
   never-overstate gate enumerates live and walks only **6 of 115 registered jurisdictions** (181
   solves). **109 jurisdictions — including Paris, Denmark, NL, Valencia, Sevilla, CH — contribute
   zero gate solves; `computeParisEnvelope` is never walked by any gate.** Gate-enumerability is
   the strongest as-built argument for data-over-procedure, and the migration pressure should be
   exactly that: a pack moves from procedural TS to declarative data *when* that makes it
   gate-walkable, kind by kind.
7. **AS-IS (Catastro FXCC/floors/KML) enters only through the typed boundary that already
   exists** (`BuildingHeightMethod`, R2 valueBasis, `isUpperBound`, the E5/E10 channel split):
   for E10 subtraction, consolidation checks, and fabric-keyed rules (the moda input). Never a
   ceiling — the ponencia trap (surveyed-as-normative) and the 43% basement trap are documented
   with their defence families (Part IV §T2).

## 5. DO-NOT-BUILD (consolidated)

envelope engines · a per-country kernel PLUG-IN seat (four disciplined escape valves cover the

port only CGA's split/repeat idiom and rule-file packaging). Full 11-row table with owners:

## 7. PHASED ROADMAP (each phase independently shippable)

- **P1 — Vocabulary closure** (unblocks Porto): the datum family, `valueBasis` everywhere,
  `CONTEXT_AGGREGATE`/`fabricDerivedHeight` (its schema change-set is already founder-authorized),
  `BANDED_LOOKUP`, `EXPLICIT_GEOMETRY_REF`. Append-only, per-kind ADRs.
- **P2 — Kernel gaps**: difference op → holes → **inclined tops** (unlocks DE + Paris crown) →
  skeleton stage 2. Oracle-pinned, inward-bias discipline preserved.
- **P3 — Gate coverage 6→115**: migrate packs to gate-walkable data kind-by-kind, Paris first;
  mint never-understate; per-solve monotonicity gate.
- **P4 — Precedence completion**: rank-as-fact, overlay consumption, refuse-on-tie.
- **P5 — The 3D stage** with per-face rule attribution + the versioned evaluation cache.
- **P6 — XPlanGML input adapter** (DE at scale rides it) + the E8 spine feeding P1 kinds.

## 8. WHAT WOULD BREAK THIS AUDIT

(1) A fuller proposal document materially different from the brief's boxes; (2) a met European
rule whose geometry cannot compile to 2.5D + inclined tops + the 14 primitives — none was found
in the corpus, but the corpus is 10 countries deep, not 25; (3) evidence that the 6/115 gate gap
is closed by machinery this audit missed; (4) a licensing ruling that changes the ODbL/DGC
posture inside legal determinations (Part IV risk R4).
