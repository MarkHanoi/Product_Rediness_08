3. RESOLVER — NO edit to jurisdiction/nationalJurisdictionResolver.ts (deliberate; see findings §3)
=====================================================================
Australia is not in the bundled national boundary set and no prefilter bbox reaches AU longitudes
(all ≤ 55.7°E), so `resolveNationalJurisdiction` returns `no-national-candidate` for every AU point
and `resolveParcelCandidates` keeps the bbox-matched AU rows (the refusal path). Extending the
national resolver to SUB-national AU routing is the lane it defers in its own header ("E4 control
10"); it would need an AUS ADM0 polygon this lane did not source. The AU rows route through the
EXISTING resolver framework with NO rival bbox — exactly the US-<STATE> idiom.
