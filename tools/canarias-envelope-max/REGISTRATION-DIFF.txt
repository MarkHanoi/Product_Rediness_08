NOTE — `TELDE_BBOX` / `isInTelde` DO NOT EXIST YET and are DELIBERATELY NOT INVENTED HERE.
Every other registration imports its extent from a `providers/<city>Bbox.ts` that was drawn
from a measured boundary; writing a rectangle from memory would be exactly the fabricated
determination this whole adapter refuses to make elsewhere. The SIPU package ships
`EDIF.shp` in WGS84-UTM-28N, so the box should be computed from that shapefile's own extent
and reprojected — a small, sourced job, not a guess.

Telde parcel routes here. Blocks 1 and 2 are independent of it and should be applied now, so
