WHY A DIFF AND NOT A PACK
-------------------------

registered, and more than València had when València was registered as a
refusal-jurisdiction. But it is still SHORT OF A NUMERIC ENVELOPE by one

that shut Paris and the Netherlands. So the honest registration is a REFUSAL

§3 — THE ONE ITEM THAT SEPARATES A REFUSAL FROM A PACK
-------------------------------------------------------
⭐ THE A1 SUBGRADO. Título 4 Capítulo 4.1 splits CONDICIONES DE APROVECHAMIENTO
across arts. 4.1.8 / 4.1.10 / 4.1.12 (A1/3.1) / 4.1.13 (A1/3.2) / 4.1.15 (A1/4.1)
/ 4.1.17 (A1/4.2). The Estructura polygon's `calificacion` reads `A1` and nothing
finer. Without the subgrado NO aprovechamiento article can be selected, so no FAR
and no height ladder can be applied.

⛔ THIS IS A DATA GAP, NOT A LEGAL ONE. The law is published, article-numbered and
readable. A 28-name sweep for a subgrado-bearing typename returned HTTP 400 on all
28 (`Regulacion`, `Calificacion`, `Zonas`, `Grados`, `Subgrados`, `Alturas`,
`Fondos`, `Alineaciones`, … ) — a 400 is an unknown-typename answer, which IS
evidence, unlike a timeout. The subgrado is presumably on the calificación PLAN
SHEET (tomo 11, 242 MB) — see §4.

⚠ CORRECTION. This paragraph previously read "20–26 distinct stroke classes
  (width × dash × grey)" and (b) below previously asserted the sheets were

  matched only the `RG`/`G` operators while these sheets paint through an

⛔ TWO THINGS BLOCK IT, BOTH NAMEABLE:
   (a) NO GEOREFERENCE. The PDF carries no CRS. Placing the sheet in EPSG:25830

   (b) THE LEGEND CANNOT DISAMBIGUATE ITSELF. Colour DOES carry zone identity
