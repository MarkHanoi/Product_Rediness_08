## ⚠ Read this first — it changes how much work this is

**1 · A BLANK IS A VALID ANSWER, AND A VALUABLE ONE.** Clau 13a legitimately has *no* FAR and *no*
height table. We recorded that as a finding and shipped the pack anyway. **If the ordinance is silent
on a field, write "none stated" and move on.** Do not hunt for a number that isn't there.

**2 · PARTIAL ANSWERS SHIP.** Our schema treats every field as independently nullable, so **a pack
with one real table and four honest blanks is worth most of its points immediately.** You do not need
to complete a clau before it is useful.

**3 · WE NEED THE SENTENCE, NOT THE SUMMARY.** A verbatim quote in Catalan/Spanish plus the article
number. A paraphrase we cannot cite is worth nothing to us, because every number we display carries
its ordinance reference to a professional user.

**4 · IF A RULE IS A PROCEDURE, WE WANT THE PROCEDURE.** Art. 242.2 does not state a buildable depth
— it states how to *derive* one ("leaving at least 30% of the block free", capped 30 m, floored 11 m).
We implement algorithms. **Do not convert a formula into a worked example.**

**5 · INE 08019 MATTERS.** Municipalities layer their own *modificacions* onto shared PGM article
numbers, so **the same article states different numbers in different towns.** Everything we currently
hold came from Castelldefels (08056), Badalona (08015), Sant Cugat (08194) or Badia del Vallès
(08904). **We need Barcelona's.**

# ③ CLAU 12 — *Nucli Antic de Substitució de l'Edificació* · 9.5% · **+17.3 cumulative**

### ⚠⚠ THE SCOPE QUESTION — get this right or the whole pack is wrong

Art. 315.2 says subzona I (clau 12) applies to *"tots els nuclis antics **diferents del de
Barcelona**"*, and subzona II (12b) to the historic centre.

**"El nucli antic de Barcelona" means CIUTAT VELLA — not the municipality.** Barcelona absorbed
formerly independent towns — **Gràcia, Sarrià, Sants, Sant Andreu, Horta** — and each has its own
*nucli antic*, which are literally *"nuclis antics diferents del de Barcelona"*. **So clau 12 governs
those, inside Barcelona.** Our own zoning query confirms it: **26 clau-12 points spread across the
municipality against 5 of 12b clustered tightly on Ciutat Vella.**

*(An earlier research pass read this the other way and advised deleting clau 12 from our roadmap —
which would have removed a tenth of the city. Please sanity-check our reading.)*

### ✅ What we already hold

- **Depth is an ALGORITHM**, like Art. 242.2: *"la profunditat edificable resultarà d'una ocupació, a
  l'alçada reguladora, del **seixanta per cent (60 per 100)** de la superfície de l'illa."*
- **Art. 318** = *Nombre màxim d'habitatges per parcel·la.*
- One height anchor: *"als solars amb una longitud de façana inferior a **6,50 metres**, l'alçada
  màxima permesa mai no podrà depassar la de **10,60 metres**, corresponent a planta baixa i dos
  pisos."*

### ❌ MISSING

1. **The article number of clau 12's height *quadre*.** It sits immediately before Art. 318 —
   believed to be **Art. 317** — but we have never seen it labelled.
2. **That table's rows** — street-width bands → height and storeys.
3. **Edificabilitat.** One unverified figure of **1,40 m²st/m²s at Art. 316** is circulating; we have
   not confirmed it and will not use it unconfirmed.
