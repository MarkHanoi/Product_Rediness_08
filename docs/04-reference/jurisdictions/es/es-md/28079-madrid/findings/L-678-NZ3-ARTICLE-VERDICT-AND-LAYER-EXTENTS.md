## §1 — The two articles, verbatim

### Art. 8.3.1 «Definición general» — PDF p. 397 (printed 395)

> «Corresponde a áreas de suelo urbano donde, totalmente o en su mayor parte, se considera
> concluido el proceso de ocupación del espacio y, consecuentemente, en la misma medida **se ha
> agotado el aprovechamiento urbanístico**.
>
> En esta zona el Plan General asume la consolidación de la ciudad resultante de la aplicación de
> una diversidad de instrumentos y regulaciones de ordenación pormenorizada precedentes, dispersos
> en el espacio y en el tiempo, **sin imponer un nuevo modelo** pero articulando medidas para la
> adecuación, mejora funcional y renovación del tejido urbano existente.»

*(Chapter and article both modified by MPG 00/335, aprobación definitiva 10.05.2016, BOCM
19.05.2016 — footnotes 499/500.)*

### Art. 8.3.5 «Obras admisibles» — PDF pp. 399–403 (printed 397–401)

⚠ **Its section heading is load-bearing and was missed the first time:**

> «**Sección Primera: Condiciones de edificación del Grado 1º**»
> «Artículo 8.3.5 **Obras admisibles**»

The article title is *admissible **works***, not *conditions of the zone*. Its subject is what may
be done **to a building that already stands**.

> **8.3.5.3.a).i)** «La nueva edificación deberá inscribirse dentro de la **envolvente exterior del
> edificio existente**, constituida por sus cubiertas, fachadas exteriores y, en su caso,
> medianeras, y **respetará la superficie total edificada del mismo**.»

## §2 — Why they do not conflict, and why 8.3.1 governs

**Every quantity Cap. 8.3 states is anchored to the existing building or to the antecedent
instrument. There is no free parameter anywhere in the chapter.** Five independent confirmations,
all verbatim from the filed PDF:

| # | Article | Verbatim anchor | Page |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **8.3.5.1** | increases admissible only «cuando no ocasionen la superación de la edificabilidad permitida, **entendiendo como tal la superficie edificada total del edificio existente**» | 399 |
| 2 | **8.3.5.3.a).i)** | «dentro de la **envolvente exterior del edificio existente** … **respetará la superficie total edificada del mismo**» | 399 |
| 3 | **8.3.5.3.a).i)**, *Estudio de Detalle* route | «– **No sobrepasar la edificabilidad permitida, entendida ésta como la superficie edificada total del edificio existente.**» | 400 |
| 4 | **8.3.5.4** | «la superficie edificada existente podrá distribuirse y situarse en cualquier parte de la edificación, **salvo cuando el planeamiento antecedente hubiese establecido expresamente una situación vinculante**» | 403 |
| 5 | **8.3.3.1.b)** | «**Salvo que del planeamiento antecedente se concluyera su condición de parcelas edificables, los suelos vacantes tendrán la consideración de espacios libres SIN APROVECHAMIENTO URBANÍSTICO.**» | 398 |

And **grado 2º** routes the same way — Art. 8.3.2.3: buildable parcels may exist, «**conforme a las
referidas ordenaciones específicas asumidas por el Plan General de 1997**».

**The reconciliation, plainly.** Art. 8.3.5 is a *substitution / obras* regime that **presupposes an
existing building** and adopts **that building's own envelope and built area** as the measure. It
does not grant a zone envelope; it is Art. 8.3.1 restated operationally — *the entitlement is
exactly what already stands, because it was consumed by earlier instruments*. **Both articles are
true simultaneously, and neither yields a municipal zone envelope.**

### The vacant-parcel case, which is where a zone envelope would have to come from

It does not come. Art. 8.3.5.3.b) — the only new-build route on unbuilt NZ-3 land:

> «b) Obras de nueva planta: i) En espacios libres no dotacionales (espacios interbloque). **Con
> carácter excepcional** podrán autorizarse obras de nueva planta … destinadas a: • Servicios
> infraestructurales y garajes-aparcamientos: **En edificación enteramente subterránea**, salvo los
> dispositivos y construcciones auxiliares imprescindibles para su funcionalidad. La superficie
> sobre rasante deberá destinarse a espacio libre acondicionado como área estancial.» *(p. 400)*

Exceptional, restricted to infrastructure and parking, and **entirely below ground**. Combined with
8.3.3.1.b) (vacant land defaults to *espacios libres sin aprovechamiento urbanístico*), **there is
no NZ-3 parcel for which the PGOUM-97 states a buildable volume of its own.**

### ⚠ The trap, restated because it survives this verdict

Art. 8.3.5.3.a).i) *does* let a substituted volume borrow «los parámetros … de la **Norma Zonal 5**
… y de la **Norma Zonal 8, Grado 4º**», and *«acogerse a la **ocupación y altura** de este grado»*.
Read alone, that is an invitation to pack NZ 3 from NZ 5/NZ 8 and book 60 % of Madrid. **Every one
of those borrowings sits beneath row 3 of the table above.** Applying NZ 5's ocupación and altura
without the existing-GFA ceiling is the L-616 mechanism-A over-statement across 60.458 % of the
city — a two-storey villa issued a NZ-5 bulk envelope.

## §3 — Doctrine-B extent check: the geometry is NOT city-wide, and one layer is not what it looks like

| **6** | *Detalle de la edificación* | 119 552 | 21.99 × 28.94 | — |
| **12** | *Fondo* | **1** | 31.27 × 36.79 | full municipal envelope |

Two conclusions, both measured:

1. **Layers 2 / 8 / 9 are NOT city-wide.** They occupy a ~4.7 × 6.2 km central window — consistent
   with **APE.00.01** scoping. Under ADR-0283, *partial publication authorises nothing beyond its
   demonstrated spatial extent*: even had the law permitted an envelope, coverage could have been
   claimed on **≈5 %** of Madrid, never on 60 %.
2. ⭐ **Layer 12 «Fondo» is ONE feature spanning the whole municipality.** A single municipality-wide
   polygon cannot be a per-parcel *fondo edificable*; it is the cartographic **background/mask**
   (`Fondo` = *background* in Spanish map-series usage), which is also why it sits beside
   *Cartografía*, *Malla 2000* and *Línea de Término*. **The "layer 12 is the fondo published as
   geometry" hypothesis is eliminated on a count of 1.**

⚠ **`returnDistinctValues` returned `[]` for `TIPOANEDIF`, `PROTECCION`, `PLANO_AE` and `ENLACE`**,
on layers whose schemas declare those fields and which advertise `supportsDistinct: true`. **That is
UNKNOWN — a query-interface limit — NOT "the fields are empty."** No conclusion is drawn from it,
and none is needed: the article verdict makes the attribute question moot for NZ 3.

## §4 — What this closes, and what it does not

**CLOSES, permanently:** *"is any part of NZ 3 orderable directly?"* — **No.** Not on any parcel, in
either grado, by any of the four *obra* types. `madridNZ3Refusal` is **correct, legally grounded,
and final**, and under C63 §1.5 / L-656 its `not-determined` tier is the right score. Row 13's
closure condition is now one statement a human can sign in a sitting, not a research programme.

**DOES NOT CLOSE:**
- The **human signature** on Cap. 8.3 (row 13's remaining half). Everything above is a reading by a
  machine of a document a machine also transcribed. It is now cheap to check: one chapter, filed
  in-repo, with the five anchors tabulated.
- **Supersession** — Cap. 8.3 carries MPG 00/335 (BOCM 19.05.2016) and Arts. 8.3.2/8.3.3/8.3.4/8.3.5
  additionally carry MPG 00/343 (BOCM 27.11.2023). **Neither BOCM text is held.** A 2023 amendment
  to the very articles this verdict rests on is exactly the risk ADR-0283 exists for.
- **NZ 4's datum** (row 7) — layer 2 is a genuine *«Fondo máximo para nueva planta o
  reestructuración general»* with 1 785 features. It is real, it is published, and it covers ≈4.9 %
  of the city. That is a lead for **NZ 4**, on its own extent, and nothing to do with NZ 3.
