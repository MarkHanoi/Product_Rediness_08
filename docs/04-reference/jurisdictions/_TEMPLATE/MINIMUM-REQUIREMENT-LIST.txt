THE ABSOLUTE MINIMUM FOR YOUR 10/10 TEST

If we want to simplify all of this into a hard pass/fail test, I would use these 15 mandatory information groups:

1. Parcel geometry
2. Parcel area
3. Applicable planning instrument
4. Current/legal plan status
5. Zoning polygon
6. Zoning/category
7. Permitted use
8. Maximum floor area / edificability
9. Maximum site coverage / footprint
10. Maximum height
11. Maximum floors
12. Setbacks/alignment/building depth
13. Relevant overlays and constraints
14. Conditional/override rules
15. Legal provenance + currentness
