## PART 2 — "the hard boundary" (the reachability boundary follow-up)

[PART 2 — "the hard boundary": 🔴 truly unreachable: RNU→PAU per-parcel; future authority decision; ABF discretion; qualitative interpretation ("harmonious" → never manufacture 8.7m); legal party-wall (mitoyen) status from GIS. 🟠 reachable-not-computable: the height number (XML/text/PDF/graphic); exact setback distance (rule vs graphic); height datum semantics; GRAPHIC rules (REGLEMENT_GRAPHIQUE, dimensions drawn not encoded — reachable ≠ structured, CV/diagram parsing needs validation). 🟡 ambiguous: plan-masse sector ≠ guaranteed 3D volume (associated material holds it); OAP = orientation/principle/intentions not numeric envelope; annexes (sanitation/flood/archaeology/noise/captages/forest…) extensive but not uniformly schematized — some national APIs excellent, others PDF map + explanatory doc. 🟢 NOT-a-gap list: parcel, zone, document/version/date, destinations, prescription existence/type/geometry, SUP, terrain, buildings, road geometry, heritage constraints, risk layers, historical permits, transactions. Five-state spec table: 🟢 source-complete / 🔵 derivable / 🟡 extractable / 🟠 interpretive / 🔴 undeterminable. Hard-red list is short: RNU→PAU, future permit, future ABF, discretion, party-wall, numeric-for-qualitative, unpublished interpretation. Caveat: CNIG multiple versions (PLU/CC v2022-10 + v2025-06, legacy retained); real GPU packages vary substantially. Two claims distinguished: "France publishes it somewhere" (very strong) vs "PRYZM can retrieve+parse automatically for every parcel" (needs measurement). Boundary estimate: ~80-90% directly accessible or deterministically derivable; most of the remaining 10-20% accessible but needs document/rule extraction; small residual genuinely non-computable. Do NOT put percentages in the investor spec — measure via the 100-parcel per-field trace audit with failure labels missing-source/inaccessible/PDF/graphic/semantic/discretionary.
