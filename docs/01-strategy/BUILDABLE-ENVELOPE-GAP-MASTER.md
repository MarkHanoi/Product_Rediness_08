# Buildable Envelope Gap — Master

> **What this is.** ONE decision document. For every jurisdiction PRYZM touches it answers four
> questions: *does PRYZM draw a cited buildable envelope today, does it refuse honestly, or is
> there no coverage yet — what concrete thing closes the gap, who owns it (US = build / DATA =
> source-or-credential / FOUNDER = decision-or-signature), and how long does it take.* It is
> assembled from the five region files under `envelope-gap-master/` (Nordic·Baltic·British,
> Iberia·France, Central-EU, South-East-EU, Beyond-Europe), deduplicated and made consistent.
> Read-only synthesis of a probe-verified evidence base — the probing is done and committed; every
> claim in the per-jurisdiction blocks cites its evidence file.
>
> **Vocabulary is the founder's own.** The 33 attributes **A1–F8** and the irreducible **8-field
> core** (`STR-ENVELOPE-PARAMETER-REFERENCE.md`); the three functional slots **Ω / P / V**, the
> substitution paths **P1–P5 / V1–V6**, the Sufficiency Legends **L0–L7** and the **A.5 degradation
> ladder** (`STR-ENVELOPE-SUFFICIENCY-LEGENDS.md`); the source hierarchy **P1–P6**, the three
> envelope-information **Types A / B / C**, and the **consume-before-compute** doctrine —
> *"never calculate what the government already publishes as geometry"* (`STR-EUROPEAN-ENVELOPE-SOURCES.md` §8–9).
>
> **How to read it.** §1 the executive table (scan the whole picture). §2 the consume-now board
> (the cheapest envelopes on the planet — geometry already published, gap is wiring not building).
> §3 the full per-jurisdiction blocks by region. §4 the gap taxonomy (the shared work). §5 the
> ordered build plan (what to build first, tied to P1–P6). The document is a decision aid, not a log.

## The one frame that governs everything below

**No government on earth serves a *composed legal buildable-envelope determination* — 21/21 European
countries confirmed, and it holds beyond Europe too** (memory `europe-e5-reuse-verdict`;
`E5-DATA-REUSE-REPORT.md` §A.2). That determination — `parcel ∩ footprint ∩ height ∩ coverage ∩
overlays`, with the datum resolved, the overlays subtracted in order, and a cited refusal wherever a
number cannot be had — **is PRYZM's IP.** What the best jurisdictions serve is the *inputs*: the drawn
footprint/line **geometry** (Type A), and/or the **numeric parameters** (Type B). So "consume not
build" always means *consume the inputs* — the intersection, the datum resolution, the overlay order,
the refusal logic and the provenance stay ours. Every "DRAWS" below is a composition PRYZM performs
over consumed inputs, gated by a human signature (L-449); a pack may never sign its own transcription.

**The single most important measured fact:** the cheapest envelopes are not in Europe. Two Australian
states (South Australia, NSW) and a Gulf/US handful serve height, FAR, coverage, setbacks — some even
a "Building Envelope" polygon — **as data**, refuting the "development potential is served nowhere"
pattern for the *inputs*. The loudest wins, ranked, are §2.

## 2 · The consume-now board — the cheapest envelopes, ranked

These are the places where the authority **already publishes the envelope geometry and/or the
numbers as machine-readable data**, so the gap is *wiring, not building* — the founder's §8 doctrine
paying off. Ranked loudest first (coverage × licence-clarity ÷ effort). The refrain throughout:
**never recompute what the state already draws.**

1. **France — the GPU, the single loudest CONSUME win in Europe.** The Géoportail de l'Urbanisme
   publishes the geometric *consequence* of the rules nationally, keyless, Licence Ouverte 2.0:
   **69,739 drawn setback lines + 51,407 setback zones + 5,291 plan-masse sectors (the buildable
   volume as a polygon) + 61,176 height-limit polygons**, each with the `idurba` plan-version join.
   The consumer is already built (`countryAdapters/fr/frPrescriptionGeometry.ts`) and Paris proves
   the shape end-to-end. **Gap: national routing only** (ranked #1 CONSUME-NOW, score 1340). Since
   France abolished the COS in 2014, the drawn envelope *is* the entitlement.
2. **South Australia (Adelaide) — the best buildable-envelope jurisdiction found anywhere, Europe
   included.** PlanSA/SAPPA serves Maximum Building Height (metres **and** levels), Minimum
   Primary/Side/Rear Setback, Site Coverage, Minimum Frontage, Minimum Site Area **and a dedicated
   "Building Envelope" polygon** — Type B parameters *and* Type A geometry together. A near-pure
   consume pack → an Adelaide envelope in **days**. `BuildableEnvelope = Building Envelope ∩ height ∩
   coverage ∩ setbacks`, all served.
3. **Netherlands — the proof consume-before-compute ships.** `bouwvlak` (the buildable footprint
   polygon) + `maatvoering` (`maximum bouwhoogte`), nationwide, keyless, **certified & LIVE**
   (`NL_BESTEMMINGSPLAN_CERTIFIED`, signed 2026-08-26). It already draws. Remaining work is a
   completeness sweep (file the free DSO key), not building.
4. **Denmark — the Type-A exemplar.** `byggefelt` (57,080 fields) + `maxetager` + `maxbygnhjd` +
   `eareal`, keyless Plandata WFS, **already draws end-to-end** behind a founder-signed rule pack.
   The whole strategy rests on this proof.
5. **Albania ⭐⭐ — the best data-to-effort ratio in Europe's south-east.** A national keyless ArcGIS
   FeatureServer serves **91,939 structural-unit polygons with max height (storeys AND metres), FAR
   (`intesitet`), coverage (`ksht`) AS FIELDS**, feature-proven. **Blocked only on one email** — the
   AKPT licence (`licenseInfo` empty). ~3–5 days once licenced; "one email could move it ten places."
6. **New South Wales (Sydney) — Type B height + FSR served as data.** Height of Building (m) and
   Floor Space Ratio as polygon attributes with the legislative clause attached (`MAX_B_H_M: 22.0`,
   `FSR: 3.5`). Consume the two layers → a height+FSR-bounded envelope in **days**; footprint follows
   via the degradation ladder until council DCP setbacks land.
7. **New York City — the D1 yield served as data.** MapPLUTO serves `ResidFAR/CommFAR/FacilFAR +
   ZoneDist1 + LotArea` per tax-lot — the strongest US Type-B case. Half the yield math is free; the
   work is the L4 sky-exposure-plane shaping engine (already in-flight) + the ZR district table.
8. **Slovenia — consume the lines, refuse the numbers.** 10,869 `REG_CRTE` "Gradbena meja"
   building-boundary lines (CC BY 4.0, keyless, plan-linked, validity-dated) + parcel routing live.
   ⛔ The numeric slots are **sentinel-zero at ~1% fill** — ship footprint-with-unresolved-height, and
   refuse coverage/FAR until the municipal PIP text is extracted.
9. **Estonia — the single cheapest new envelope after Denmark.** `hoonestusala` polygons + the
   fullest numeric tuple in the Nordic-Baltic region (`tihedus` FAR · `protsent` coverage · `korgus`
   height · `sbp` GFA), keyless, and **the adapter already fetches the polygon**. The gap is ONE
   function: the EE→C58 join. ~2–4 days.
10. **Luxembourg — the cheapest full adapter in Europe, built, blocked on two named things.** One CC0
    national GeoPackage holds 653,315 parcels + 46,191 zones + **2,442 building lines** + COS/CUS/CSS
    at 93.7% fill. Adapter code built. Needs a usable parcel key (`NUM_CADAST` is not one) + a founder
    signature on the mapping. Days.
11. **Switzerland (Zürich → the cantons).** Per-canton FAR (Nutzungsziffer × parcel area → GFA cap),
    **certified & live at Zürich**; each additional canton is a transcription of its per-code table +
    one signature — never new machinery. Height stays honestly refused (cantonal Baureglement PDF).
12. **Lithuania — a second national byggefelt, live and keyless.** `Statybos zona` (36,476 polygons +
    `MAX_AUK_M`) + `Statybos linija` (build-to line), keyless. Resolve the `MAX_INTENS` units
    question, then the same consume-join shape as Estonia. ~3–5 days.
13. **Iceland — the highest-value greenfield in the Nordic-Baltic region.** `byggingarreitur` polygons
    + floors + `byggmagn` (GFA) on a keyless HMS ArcGIS feed nobody had wired. Build the IS adapter
    (the EE shape). ~1 week, no gate, no purchase.
14. **Croatia — a screening mask, not the legal envelope.** 89,911 construction-area polygons,
    keyless — but explicitly **"not for issuing legal acts"** (2020 vintage). Consume as a
    buildable/non-buildable Ω-mask with the caveat; ~2–3 days once the founder ratifies a screening
    tier. Do not confuse with the envelope.
15. **Qatar + Israel — Type A zone/plan geometry served.** Qatar's zone polygons (`ZONING`, `RULEID`)
    and Israel's iplan plan land-use polygons (`pl_number`) are served as data — zone identity and
    footprint context free; only the numeric tables need extraction (with a perfect join key).
16. **Madrid NZ-1 + the 29,105 official alignment lines.** The NZ-1 explicit-geometry ring already
    draws (signed); the 29,105 `Alineación Oficial` lines are one probe from consumption into the same
    registered `explicit-area` pack. (Do **not** consume the fondo layer 12 — semantics unverified.)
17. **Bulgaria-Sofia — the construction-boundary line + zone-coded polygons** are live keyless on the
    Sofiaplan ArcGIS server; the only build is transcribing the ЗУЗСО annex numeric tables, then the
    code→number join is deterministic.

**The through-line:** items 1–4 already draw or are one routing step away; 5–13 are a small
consume-join or one credential; 14–17 are consume-with-a-caveat or one-probe-away. None needs a new
engine. **The composed determination is served nowhere — but the inputs, increasingly, are.**

### ▸ REGION — Nordic · Baltic · British


#### REGION HEADLINE — read this first

**This is the world capital of Type-A envelope geometry.** The census verdict is blunt:
*"the Nordic-Baltic region is the world capital of Type-A envelope geometry — DK, EE, LT, IS serve
building-field polygons today, keyless; SE has it gated, NO has it render-only, FI has it
modelled. Only LV lacks the geometry axis entirely"* (`census-nordic-baltic.md`, Lane synthesis).
Four countries publish the **drawn permitted footprint as machine-readable features** — the exact
thing the founder's §8 says never to recompute: *"Never calculate what the government already
publishes as geometry."*

**One critical honesty caveat that governs the whole region.** The *composed legal envelope
determination* is still state-served NOWHERE — that is PRYZM's IP, holding 21/21 across Europe
(memory `europe-e5-reuse-verdict`; E5-DATA-REUSE-REPORT). What the four countries serve is the
**building-field polygon (Type A geometry) plus the numeric parameters** — the P-slot and much of
the V-slot as data. PRYZM's job is to CONSUME that and compose `byggefelt ∩ height ∩ coverage ∩
restrictions` (STR §8). So "consume not build" means consume the *inputs*; the intersection and the
refusal logic are still ours.

**Where the money is:** Denmark already draws end-to-end and is the exemplar. Estonia, Lithuania,
Iceland serve the same class of geometry keyless — the gap there is a small **consume-join**, not a
rule-pack build. Sweden/Norway/Finland have the geometry but behind a free-registration gate, an
agreement gate, or a not-yet-open channel. Latvia has no geometry axis but the cleanest licence
(CC0) and a clean text-extraction target. Great Britain is the honest exception: **L7 discretionary,
no by-right envelope exists to draw — the deliverable is a range, never a number.** Ireland is
structured-zoning + part-discretionary — the WHERE is served, the HOW-MUCH is in PDFs.

## 4 · Gap taxonomy — the shared work (fix once, unblock many)

The per-jurisdiction gaps collapse into nine recurring shapes. Grouping them is how the founder sees
that most of the map is closed by a handful of investments, not by 75 bespoke fights.

### G1 · Missing height channel (the V-slot)
Footprint/line geometry served, but no height number: **Luxembourg** (open-top footprint — height in
the QE-zone DOCX), **Slovenia** (sentinel-zero numerics), **Poland** (FAR/coverage withheld on the
denominator), **Switzerland** (height not in the WFS/INTERLIS — cantonal Baureglement PDF),
**Iceland** (floors served, metres unknown), **Czechia**, **NSW** (footprint), **Germany** (thin
`hoehenangabe` fill). *Shared fix:* per-jurisdiction height sourcing (Type-B where served, P4
extraction where not); ship the A.5 rung *"footprint derived; vertical extent unresolved"* meanwhile,
never a fabricated cap.

### G2 · Missing datum (A2) — schema seat CLOSED, resolution still owed
Every vertical limit is measured *from* somewhere. **ADR-0377 (ACCEPTED 2026-09-02, lane S1) closed
the representation gap** — a stated height now names its datum, and absence = `unknown` = refuse. What
remains is *resolution to metres*: **Germany** (`72,2 m über NHN` needs a national DTM/NHN grid),
**Paris** (HMC is NGF-absolute → needs terrain to become a cap), **every beyond-Europe city** (a
per-city geoid-lift constant — the AU swing is 84 m Perth→Darwin, so no national constant is
possible), **Albania** (no datum on the layer). Barcelona's façade-rasant scar (L-584) is the one
already closed. Terrain *rendering* is a blocker nowhere (Mapterhorn adopted planet-wide); this is the
datum *number*, not the mesh.

### G3 · Missing rule-pack extraction (P4 documents) — the dominant, most-shared cost
Identity and often geometry are served; the numbers live in text/PDF. This is the single largest
surface and it is the *same problem* almost everywhere: **Portugal** (regulamento — cércea/índice/
pisos/afastamento), **France** (règlement — hauteur/marges/emprise), the **Spanish CA tail**
(ordinance PDFs), **Latvia** (TIAN HTML), **Ireland** (dev-plan standards), **Italy** (per-comune
NTA at ~9–11% fill), **Greece** (FEK scans), **Bulgaria/Cyprus** (ЗУЗСО / DTPH coefficient tables),
and most of **Australia + US + the ME trio**. *Shared fix:* **one provenance-first extraction
pipeline keyed on the unified identity code** — France (`idurba`) and Portugal (CRUS 18-category) are
the identical shape, and it is exactly the NREL-COMPASS / Catala accelerator territory. Australia is
the cheapest instance (one state-wide document — WA SPP 7.3, TAS SPP, ACT Territory Plan, VIC
clause-32 — covers every coded parcel). Numbers are **never inferred**; where the text says no number
exists → a `discretionary` range, never interpolation.

### G4 · Missing credential / licence / access gate — data exists, behind a gate
Ranked by ease of clearing: **free registration** (Sweden NGP) < **free key/form** (Finland MML, NL
DSO) < **data-permit** (Finland Ryhti) < **one email** (Albania AKPT, Croatia, Qatar/Israel/Tasmania
licences) < **agreement** (Norway Norge digitalt) < **purchase** (WA Landgate cadastre) < **commercial
relationship** (Hungary Lechner, Saudi Balady SSO) < **vantage/geo-fence/WAF** (Dubai, Abu Dhabi UAE
PASS, Malta Cloudflare, Serbia/N.Macedonia/Moldova). *Plus the universal beyond-Europe wiring:* the
`/api/parcel/*` server proxies are unwired, so today a US/AU/ME click falls to the OSM footprint even
where the parcel is probe-verified. Most of these are **not engineering** — they are a signature, a
form, or a probe from the right IP.

### G5 · Missing schema / kernel capability — the S1 seats CLOSED three; the kernel still owes two
**Already closed (schema seats, all ACCEPTED 2026-09-02, lane S1):** **ADR-0377** datum-naming
(A2/G2); **ADR-0378** `height-proportional-offset` — the GeometricRule kind for Germany's
*Abstandsflächen* (0,4·H, min 3 m), evaluated post-height; **ADR-0379** `context-aggregate` — a
fabric statistic as a rule value, i.e. the `fabricDerivedHeight` seat named by Porto's *moda da
cércea* blocker, and the vehicle for Italy's DM 1444 neighbour-facing rule and ES neighbour-derived
heights. **Still open (build once, many served):**
- **The K1 geometry-kernel inclined-plane / height-field-top primitive** (`geometry/inclinedTop.ts`,
  written but UNTRACKED). The *evaluator* the three schema seats hand work to. It serves Denmark's
  `skrå højdegrænseplan`, German §6, the **Paris** crown, Barcelona/ES **coronación**, **NYC**'s
  sky-exposure plane and the Nordic solar-access planes — **six+ jurisdictions from one primitive.**
- **The polygon-difference kernel op (A ∖ B)** — Sweden's `prickmark` is *parcel minus no-build
  strips*; the kernel ships intersection + union only. Unlocks 11,662 Swedish plans once NGP opens.
- **`BuildableEnvelope.footprintIsRequired`** — the MIN/binding side of a Danish `byggefelt`
  (`bygkunifelt = true`); needs a superseding ADR over C58 §2.4 (US + FOUNDER). Drawing a binding
  field as merely permitted *understates* the obligation.
- **Small per-jurisdiction kinds:** Murcia/Marseille **A3 per-edge frontage** classification;
  Marseille **graphic-primacy precedence**; Madrid **NZ-5 street-centreline separation**; Lisbon
  **`transferableRights`** overlay; Italy **same-plot distance-between-buildings** seat + A6
  neighbour-height *input* (Italy has no national LoD1 product for it).

**The headline for the founder:** the frozen architecture already carries every hard construction
these jurisdictions throw at it — 20/30 populated West hard-case cells PASS today, and **not one cell
needs a new constraint graph or a per-country engine.** The remaining kernel work is the ONE inclined-
plane primitive plus the A∖B op.

### G6 · Discretionary / L7 — no envelope exists in law (the honest boundary)
Where there is no by-right envelope, the honest deliverable is a **range with a stated basis, never a
number**: **Great Britain** (England/Scotland/Wales — discretionary consent, BRE daylight tests),
**Houston** (no zoning *at all* — L7 by absence, not discretion; deed-restriction + Chapter 42
governed), **Sydney central "CA" clause-areas** (the polygon says numeric control doesn't apply),
**Ireland**'s part-discretionary residue. The providers already refuse correctly. Building the range
engine + the constraint-intersection layer is the entire product here — and for GB it is a **FOUNDER
product decision** (Product-A), not an engineering one.

### G7 · Screening-grade / not-for-legal-acts — a pre-filter, never the envelope
Data that constrains the *domain* but is not the legal determination: **Croatia**'s 2020
construction-area mask, **Andorra**'s geological-hazard WFS (a negative overlay), **Albania**'s
road/rail/heritage restriction polygons (the H-subtraction step), Spain's **SIU** (used as a refusal
trigger, never a value source). Ship as an explicit screening tier with the caveat carried in the
product framing.

### G8 · Routing / boundary-wave — parcel adapter built but dormant on a resolver flip
The parcel leg is coded and live-proven but the national resolver hasn't been told the country is
claimable: **Slovenia, Slovakia, Hungary, Poland, Luxembourg, Greece, Croatia, Romania.** These move
as **batched boundary waves** (with neighbour-integrity refusal rings), not per-lane edits —
promote the country into `jurisdiction/data/nationalBoundaries.json`, add the prefilter, wire the
`/api/parcel/<cc>` proxy row.

### G9 · Signature (L-449) — a legal act reserved to a human
Every "DRAWS" is gated by a FOUNDER signature over the transcription/mapping, and **a pack may never
sign its own transcription** (the Madrid defect). Open signatures with the machinery already built:
Madrid **SIG-M1**, Murcia **SIG-MU2**, **CM-SPACM**, Canarias, **AMB per-municipality**, Paris
verifier, Luxembourg mapping, Poland POG, Germany per-Land, Switzerland per-canton. These are the
cheapest "DRAWS" flips in the whole document — hours of human decision, zero engineering.

authoritative geometry → **P2** authoritative parameters → **P3** structured rule data → **P4**
authoritative documents → **P5** deterministic inference → **P6** AI (never legal geometry without
deterministic validation). The rule that generalises the whole system: *adding a country should mean
adding a mapping from its ordering types to existing legends — not adding a branch to the engine.*

  ADR-0378 `height-proportional-offset` kind. Serves **Germany** Abstandsflächen (the gate for its

- **The one-document-per-state Australia extractions** — WA SPP 7.3, TAS SPP, ACT Territory Plan, VIC

### Phase 5 — blocked-not-absent (probe / credential / wait · watch-list)
