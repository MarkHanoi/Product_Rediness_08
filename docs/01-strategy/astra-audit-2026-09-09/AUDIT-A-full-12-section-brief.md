FINAL FEASIBILITY VERDICT\n\n> ### **TECHNICALLY POSSIBLE BUT PREMATURE** — for the capability as the founder framed it (unstructured input → AI interpretation → trusted semantic BIM).\n\n**Defence.** The arrow is premature at both ends, and the middle is not the problem.\n\n*At the input end,* nobody has demonstrated it. There is **zero** evidence — after four distinct targeted searches — of any frontier model ingesting a real construction drawing set or understanding IFC. OpenAI's own architecture demo took a **text brief** as input and produced floor plans as an *output*; every drawing-in example is a user demo on clean concept artwork, none independently reproduced. The headline BenchCAD 95.9% is mechanical parts from clean synthetic renders, scored on geometric overlap — the metric that benchmark's own authors warn diverges from parametric fidelity. The nearest measured architectural proxies are damning: **22% line-endpoint PCK** for frontier VLMs on cross-view plan/section grounding, and MCP4IFC — the published LLM→IFC system — fails on exactly *walls not connecting at intersections, openings not properly added, no IfcSpace, missing property sets*. That is C15, C84 and the room graph, item for item.\n\n*At the output end,* the measured ceiling for LLMs manipulating IFC at all is **49.5% mean / ≤3.4% strict solve** (BIM-Edit, arXiv 2606.20146, June 2026) — on **editing** an IFC that already has semantic structure, which is strictly easier than reconstruction. That is the out-of-the-box floor for an unassisted model, not the ceiling for a constrained pipeline; but the constrained-pipeline number **has never been measured by anyone**, including PRYZM.\n\n*In the middle,* PRYZM is stronger than the founder believes and its gaps are wiring, not research: 34 L0 element schemas with provenance slots on 32 of 32 kinds (hard-0 gate, executed planted-defect controls), ~12 `*.batch.create` verbs, a working `WallGraph`/`PlanarTopologyEngine`, manifold-3d CSG, bidirectional IFC. What is missing is not capability. It is **measurement** — and a company cannot stake a product on an accuracy it has never computed.\n\n\"Premature\" is therefore precise: not *wrong*, not *impossible*, **not yet decidable**. The evidence needed to decide it is 30 days of corpus work, not two quarters of pipeline work.\n\n---\n\n## B.
