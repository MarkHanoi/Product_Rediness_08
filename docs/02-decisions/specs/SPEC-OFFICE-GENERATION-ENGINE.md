# SPEC — Office Generation Engine

Status: **Active** · Owner: founder-directed · Governs: `apps/editor/src/ui/office-building/*`,
`packages/ai-host/src/workflows/officeBuilding/*`, a new `packages/ai-host/src/workflows/officeFurnish/*`.

The end goal is a **best-in-class generative office engine** producing realistic, code-compliant,
efficient workplace layouts that rival experienced workplace architects. Benchmark planning
principles (not designs) against Gensler / HOK / Perkins&Will / UNStudio and workstation systems
from Steelcase / Herman Miller / Haworth / MillerKnoll: workstation density, circulation, collaboration,
acoustic zoning, flexibility.

Implemented in COMMITTED PHASES (resilient to session restarts). Each phase gates root
`tsc --skipLibCheck --noEmit` = 0 and ships independently.

---

## 1. Split into two independent systems

The office generator is divided into two completely independent stages. **Architecture is never
regenerated by furnishing; furnishing only adds furniture.**

### Command 1 — Generate Office Architecture (architectural shell ONLY)
MUST create: building façade · external walls · internal partition walls · roof · doors · windows ·
curtain walls · structural core · lift shafts · staircases · toilets · accessible toilets ·
kitchenette rooms · main kitchenette cabinetry (built-in casework) · service rooms · plant rooms ·
storage rooms · circulation corridors.

MUST NOT create: desks · chairs · computers · reception · collaboration areas · sofas · meeting-room
furniture · lounge furniture · canteens · decorative elements · plants · office accessories.

Output = a fully functional architectural office floor.

### Command 2 — Furnish Office (interior fit-out ONLY)
A separate AI command that ANALYSES the existing architecture and populates it with: office desks ·
chairs · monitors · reception · collaboration spaces · meeting rooms · sofas · booth seating · phone
booths · kitchen furniture · breakout areas · canteen seating · informal meeting spaces · decoration ·
storage · lockers. Architecture must never be regenerated — only furniture is added.

## 3. Office core rules (never generate empty cores)
Current core = walls + one door only. Insufficient. The core MUST always include:

- **Vertical circulation:** lift shafts · staircases · fire escape stairs · fire-rated lobby.
- **Toilets** (inside or directly adjacent to the core): male · female · accessible WC · cleaning
  closet · service shaft. Cubicles scale with floor size / occupant load:
  - Small floor → 2 cubicles per gender
  - Medium floor → 3–4 per gender
  - Large floor → 5+ per gender
  - Very large → scale by occupant load.

---

## 4. Office architecture rules — solve circulation BEFORE rooms
Per floor plate, define in order: Gross Floor Area → Core position → Primary circulation → Escape
routes → Internal office blocks → Meeting rooms → Kitchenettes → Support rooms → Final partitions.
The algorithm solves circulation and egress before creating rooms.

---

## 5. Interior fit-out engine — modular, not random
Redesign as a MODULAR system of reusable office modules (not individually-placed random desks):

- **Single workstation:** desk · chair · computer.
- **Linear workstation:** multiple desks in a row · chair per desk · computer per desk · cable mgmt.
- **Bench workstation:** two desk rows back-to-back · shared power spine · shared cable tray.
- **Collaborative block:** sofas · coffee table · whiteboard · screens · plants.
- **Meeting room block:** meeting table · chairs · TV · whiteboard · storage.
- **Executive office:** large desk · visitor chairs · storage · meeting table.
- **Phone booth:** small acoustic room · desk · chair · lighting.
- **Kitchen block:** cabinetry · island · coffee machine · fridge · microwave · high table · stools.
- **Breakout block:** sofas · lounge chairs · coffee tables · plants.

---

## 6. Interior enclosed offices
The fit-out engine also generates enclosed internal offices using **glass curtain-wall systems**
(instead of opaque partitions) where appropriate, for a modern workplace with visual openness:
executive offices · meeting rooms · focus rooms · phone booths · HR rooms · interview rooms.

---

## 7. Circulation rules (furniture must never obstruct circulation)
Maintain minimum clearances; escape routes remain unobstructed:
- Main corridors ≈ 1800–2400 mm
- Secondary circulation ≈ 1200–1500 mm
- Desk clearance ≈ 900–1200 mm
- Meeting-room access ≈ 1000 mm minimum

---

## 8. Occupancy logic (scale automatically, no fixed layouts)
Estimate occupancy from usable floor area. Occupancy then drives: number of desks · meeting rooms ·
phone booths · toilets · kitchen size · collaboration areas · lockers. Everything scales automatically.

---

## 9. Layout generation pipeline
1. Analyse floor-plate geometry.
2. Place and size the building core (lifts, stairs, toilets, service shafts).
3. Generate primary circulation and fire-egress routes.
4. Allocate support spaces (meeting rooms, kitchenettes, storage, plant rooms).
5. Create internal partitions and glazed office enclosures.
6. Validate circulation and accessibility.
7. If requested, run the **Furnish Office** engine to populate the layout with modular workstation,
   collaboration, meeting, and amenity blocks.
8. Final validation: all furniture respects circulation, accessibility, and fire-egress constraints.

---

## 10. Benchmark
Incorporate proven planning principles (density, circulation, collaboration, acoustic zoning,
flexibility) from leading workplace-planning firms and workstation systems — principles, not designs.
