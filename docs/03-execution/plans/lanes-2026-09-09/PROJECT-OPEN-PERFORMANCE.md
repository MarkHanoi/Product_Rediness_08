## 5. STRUCTURAL — the seams

**Seam 1 — the open has no owner and no end.** `PlatformShell.setProjectContext(...): void` (`PlatformShell.ts:217-221`) fires the hydrate and returns; `buildPersistence.ts:337` awaits a non-thenable, `:341` emits `done @ 100 %`, `PlatformRouter.ts:1258` hides the overlay — **all before a single element is hydrated**. Returning the load promise makes the progress bar honest, gives `reportStartupBudget` a real end, and makes the whole tail schedulable. **Two costs to price before doing it:** (a) the router overlay stays up longer, so the open will *feel* slower while being identical — decide which overlay owns the window first; (b) `openProjectInflight` currently clears before the load, so today a second card-click starts a **fully concurrent** open — the very interleave `buildPersistence.ts:243-247` says the supersede branch exists to prevent. A completion promise alone converts that bug into a 30-second unresponsive gesture. **This seam needs a cancel token, not just a promise.**

**Seam 2 — the post-load broadcast.** 35 verified registrations on `pryzm-project-loaded`, ordered by boot order, coordinated by three ad-hoc timers (`setTimeout 0` → tier+PBR+swap, `setTimeout 150` → `zoomToAll`, `setTimeout 3500` → thumbnail) and two `requestIdleCallback`s. Replace with `openSession.afterLoad(name, priority, fn)` on the object Seam 1 creates, each step wrapped in `markStartupPhase('post-load:<name>')`, run under the one frame scheduler (P3). Migrate the five heavy steps first; leave the panel tail on the event. The timers encode real dependencies nobody wrote down ("after first committed frame", "after builders drained") — express those, do not just pick smaller numbers.

**Seam 3 — the open path is also an edit path.** An unedited open produces a full serialize, a ~1.4 MB deflate, a whole-container IndexedDB write, a thumbnail and a server enqueue, and burns one of 20 retained version slots. The right question is not "make the save faster" but **"are the post-load sweep's mutations AUTHORED or DERIVED?"** Wall joins, room re-detect and late attribution are re-derived identically every open; the `§LOAD-HEAL-DEGENERATE-POLYGON` path (`ProjectLoader.ts:2984-2989`) is a genuine repair that *must* persist. **Classify the mutators one at a time — `[[confident-register-rows-are-the-wrong-ones]]` applies directly.** Probe first: open the same project twice and compare the two post-sweep hashes (with rank 10's timestamp already stripped, or the comparison is meaningless).
