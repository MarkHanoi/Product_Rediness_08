## §0 — Method, and the collision this audit had to design around

Three read-only sweeps (ground layers · building tiers · vegetation/street/photoreal/BIM), each tagging every claim with `file:line`, then a verification pass against the shipped Cesium (`node_modules/cesium/Build/CesiumUnminified/index.js`, **1.143.0**) for what each Cesium class can actually clip. Nothing here was verified in a browser; §7 lists exactly what that leaves open.

**The collision.** While this audit ran, CONTEXT-EXTENT-2X (told by the orchestrator to make its five radii derive from ONE scope value) minted its own runtime type — `SiteContextScope { shape: 'circle' | 'rectangle'; radiusM?; halfWidthM?; halfDepthM? }` in `apps/editor/src/ui/geospatial/contextExtentBudget.ts:116-123` (an UNTRACKED leaf), with `CTX_SCOPE_MIN_RADIUS_M = 150`, `CTX_SCOPE_MAX_RADIUS_M = 1781`, `DEFAULT_SITE_CONTEXT_SCOPE = { circle, 1781 }` (`:145-154`), five `f(scope)` radii (`:221-238`) and `CesiumViewport.setContextScope()` (`CesiumViewport.ts:1920`, `contextScope` field `:1903`). That is the radial-CULL half of the scope. This lane owns the PERSISTED value, the polygon, the geometric cut, the globe/tileset clip and the slider. **Two scope types on the same day is exactly the one-owner failure L-645 exists to close, so this lane's L0 schema (`packages/schemas/src/site/SiteScope.ts`) adopts their field vocabulary verbatim** — a stored `SiteScope` is assignable to their `SiteContextScope` with no adapter, and Phase 2 turns their interface into a type re-export of the schema (one line, §6 F-10).

Every `distM` is measured from the SITE FRAME ORIGIN passed in (`planarMetres contextBuildings.ts:1370`), never the camera; the pan-refresh (`maybeRefreshContextOnPan :11195`, gate `shouldRefetchContextOnPan contextBuildings.ts:1637`, radius `CONTEXT_PAN_FARRING_RADIUS_M 1500 :1621`, cooldown 20 s, debounce 1200 ms `:11225`) only decides whether to re-run the whole load about a NEW origin.

**Seat.** Every ground layer seats per FEATURE on sampled terrain + its C12 §12.4 ladder offset (`GROUND_LAYER_OFFSET_M groundFeatureSeat.ts:52-65`: landuse 0.005 < parks 0.01 < roads/sea 0.02 < rail 0.022 < water 0.03), re-seated by `reseatContextGroundFeaturesForBase :8276`; buildings per footprint via `sampleGround :8599` (`GroundSampleBatcher`, ONE flight per distinct tile — §GROUND-SAMPLE-TILE-ATTRIBUTION). A geometric cut adds NO seat points beyond the cut vertices, which land in the same tiles: the pre-clip is free of terrain round-trips.

## §3 — The clip mechanisms, measured

| Mechanism | Owners (Cesium 1.143.0, shipped build) | Cuts what on 3D Site | Cost | Named limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `globe.clippingPolygons` (`ClippingPolygonCollection`, `inverse`) | `Globe` only | the terrain surface (#1, #1b) and hence the base colour (#2) | one SDF compute per collection change; per-fragment lookup | WebGL2 (`isSupported`); cut precision = mesh resolution at the edge; NO side wall produced; ≥3 positions per polygon |
| `tileset.clippingPolygons` | `Cesium3DTileset` | photoreal tiles (#19) | as above | ONE collection, ONE `inverse` per tileset ⇒ scope XOR parcel void (F-13); precision = tile mesh (C12 §7) |
| `Model.clippingPolygons` / `clippingPlanes` | `Model` (+ `ModelGraphics`) | the BIM glTF (#22) | trivial | forbidden here by decision (the subject is never cut) |
| **Geometric pre-clip** — `intersectPolygons2D` (rings), segment split (polylines) | any input geometry | #4–#8, #10–#13 | O(n·m) arrangement per straddling feature only (inside/outside features cost one containment sweep); Barcelona: ~10k footprints × ≤200-gon on the far read, once per load | inputs must be simple rings (self-intersecting ⇒ REFUSED ⇒ kept whole, F-… honesty); overlaps thinner than `COINCIDENT_M` (1 mm) read as no overlap (kernel contract) |
| **Instance-centre filter** | any point set | #15–#17 (and #18 when drawn) | one predicate per instance | a canopy whose centre is 1 m inside still overhangs the edge by its radius (2.8 m) — accepted (a tree at a slab edge does that in cityweft too); dropped count is printed |
| Material / shader discard (`u_scope` uniform, `discard` outside) | would need a custom `Appearance` per layer | in principle everything | one uniform per draw | **REJECTED**: entity materials are Cesium-owned (`ColorMaterialProperty`), a custom appearance per layer forks the render path six times, and the geometry — vertices, seats, memory, tile reads — is STILL built outside the slab, which is the opposite of "nothing outside is constructed" (C12 §13.1). |

## §5 — Caps INSIDE the scope: "within the scope should be sound — really detailed and completed"

The founder's two asks (L-13058 "double the extent" and L-645 "complete inside") meet at the caps. Every tier is NEAREST-FIRST then CAPPED, so inside a scope a cap **silently thins the rim**. The rule (C12 §13.5): a cap on a MAPPED layer is a completeness fact and, when it bites inside the scope, the product prints the numbers and the scope at which it would not (`scopeClip.ts capVerdict` / `completeScopeRadiusM`); a cap on a SYNTHESISED layer (people, synthetic lamps, canopy fill) is a density parameter and is reported as such, never as "dropped features".

**Barcelona, from L-13058's own counts** (near bbox 0.008° = 1781 m × 1336 m ≈ 2.38 km²; far 0.011° ≈ 4.50 km²):

| Layer (mapped) | Eligible in the measured extent | Density | Cap (now) | Complete radius at that density — `r·√(cap/eligible)` | Read-complete ceiling (z16) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| buildings (all tiers) | 9,902 in 4.50 km² | ~2,200 / km² | 14,000 whole-scene | **~1,420 m** (a 1781 m disc holds ~21,900 — the 14,000 budget bites at 1,420 m) | 1,781 m (`CTX_BUILDINGS_MAX_TILES_PER_FETCH 112`) |
| trees (mapped) | 2,462 in 2.38 km² | ~1,035 / km² | 3,000 | **~960 m** (a 1781 m disc holds ~10,300 — cap 3,000 bites at 960 m) | trees are read at the NEAR bbox (891 m) — beyond it they are not read at all (F-2) |
| lamps (mapped) | 1,018 in 2.38 km² | ~430 / km² | 2,400 | ~1,330 m | 891 m read (F-2) |
| roads / parks / rail / water | complete inside 891 m (no cap) | — | none | — | **891 m read** (F-2) |

**So the scope maximum at which EVERY cap AND every read is honoured, at Barcelona density, is ~891 m today — the near read, not a cap.** Beyond 891 m the ground layers, trees and lamps are simply not read; beyond ~960 m the tree cap bites; beyond ~1,420 m the building budget bites; beyond 1,781 m the building read drops to z15 and `--drop-densest-as-needed` deletes footprints. The extent lane's default of 1,781 m is therefore a COMPLETE slab for buildings only. Phase 2's honest ordering: (1) derive every layer's read from `scopeFetchHalfDeg` (F-1/F-2) so the ground layers and trees are read to the scope — cheap (flat entities; the tree tile arithmetic must be re-measured because trees are dropped below z16), (2) raise `CTX_TREES_MAX_INSTANCES` to ~10,000 (one instanced primitive — the cheapest geometry in the scene), (3) let the slider's default be the COMPUTED complete radius (`completeScopeRadiusM`) and print the verdict line at any larger setting. The building budget of 14,000 at 1,420 m is the first real GPU trade and is the founder's call (Q-1).

## §6 — Findings (F) and founder questions (Q)

- **F-1 — halfDeg in degrees of LATITUDE is applied to LONGITUDE.** `contextBboxAround(lat, lon, halfDeg)` (`contextBuildings.ts:594`) applies ONE number to both axes; a degree of longitude is cos φ shorter. At Barcelona (cos 41.4° = 0.750) the extent lane's `farFetchHalfDeg = 1781/111320` reads 1781 m N–S but only **1,336 m E–W** — the east and west rims of the new 1781 m far disc are DATA-STARVED (never fetched, so the "nearest-first" sort cannot even see them). Oslo: 50 % short. `siteScope.ts scopeFetchHalfDeg` gives the longitude-honest value (over-reads N–S by 1/cos φ, the safe side). Phase 2 item; also owed to the extent lane.
- **F-2 — the ground layers, trees and lamps are read at the NEAR bbox (891 m) while the far tier now reaches 1781 m.** A 1781 m slab would show buildings to the rim and roads/parks/rail/water/trees stopping at 891 m — the exact "not one value" defect of the retired slab, re-created by the extent lane. The scope must be the ONE source of every layer's read (C12 §13.1).
- **F-3 — sea island holes are dropped at render** (`:11759` / `:11820`); islands paint blue. Pre-existing; the scope clip must clip outer and holes separately, so fixing the hierarchy is a Phase 2 prerequisite for the sea.
- **F-4 — footways are fetched on every site and never drawn** (`:11272`, "slice 2" per `contextRoads.ts` header). Pre-existing.
- **F-5 — furniture (benches, bus stops, cycle parking) is parsed, counted and never built** (`contextFurniture.ts:136-140`). Pre-existing.
- **F-6 — the instanced far tier is not pickable** (no `contextFeatureByEntity` entry; `:11013-11017`); with the far radius at 1781 m and solid tiers capped at 891 m a growing share of visible buildings is unqueryable. Pre-existing, now larger.
- **F-7 — `CONTEXT_PAN_FARRING_RADIUS_M 1500` (`contextBuildings.ts:1621`) was not raised with the far tier (1781 m)** — the pan gate now sits INSIDE the drawn disc. Extent lane.
- **F-8 — `setContextScope` forces `loadContextBuildings(…, true)` which `clearContextBuildings()`s (`~:9949`) BEFORE the fetch resolves** — the clear-then-refetch window L-635 recorded as "blanked Madrid", reached through a new trigger. The slider's release must NOT go through a path that clears before it has the replacement (C12 §13.4: swap, never blank).
- **F-9 — `maybeRefreshContextOnPan` doc says 600 ms; code says 1200 ms** (`:11190` vs `:11225`). Cosmetic.
- **F-10 — two scope types minted on one day — RECONCILED IN PHASE 1.** `SiteContextScope` (`contextExtentBudget.ts`) vs `SiteScope` (`packages/schemas/src/site/SiteScope.ts`). After the extent lane landed (`854cbdd7` + `11426e2a`) this lane made `SiteContextScope` a type alias of the schema, pointed the leaf's `scopeOuterRadiusM` at the ONE body in `siteScope.ts` (adding only the fallback + range clamp the leaf owns), and exported `SITE_SCOPE_RANGE` — the one range object `resolveSiteScope` is handed, so a stored scope wider than the fetch extent is clamped WITH THE NUMBER STATED, never silently. Cycle-checked: `siteScope.ts` imports `@pryzm/schemas`, `@pryzm/geometry-kernel`, `boundaryProjection.ts` and `sceneEnuFrame.ts`, none of which import the context family.
- **F-11 — only land-use is coast-clipped**; parks/roads/rail/inland water draw over the sea. Pre-existing.
- **F-12 — "C12 §12.6 suppressed on photoreal" is not cited in code**; the behaviour exists (`:4520` region aborts + clears context when `photorealTilesActive`; sea gated `:3633`) but the direction is Forma-suppresses-photoreal (`tileset.show = !formaMode` `:2734`) as much as the reverse. The contract clause is true; the citation is absent.
- **F-13 — a tileset has ONE `ClippingPolygonCollection` and ONE `inverse`** ⇒ the parcel void (`inverse:false`) and a scope (`inverse:true`) are mutually exclusive on the photoreal globe. Named limit; decision in ADR-0382 §D6.
- **F-14 — `depthTestAgainstTerrain=false` is written in FOUR places** (`:2556`, `:4061`, `:4126`, `:4192`) and `enableLighting` has three writers by design (`cesiumSurfaceFraming.ts:98-105`). A scope-active state must not become a fifth writer of either.

- **Q-1 — the default scope: the COMPUTED complete radius (~900 m at Barcelona today) or the extent lane's 1781 m?** Option A: complete by default, slide out with the verdict line. Option B: 1781 m by default, with the line saying what is thinned. Recommendation: **A** — the founder's sentence has "complete" as the invariant and "double" as the experiment.
- **Q-2 — is the BIM model ever cropped?** Recommendation: **NO.** It is the subject the slab presents; the slider floor guarantees the parcel (and so the building) is inside. Cropping it would also re-open the C12 §1.5 ECEF bridge question for no visible gain.
- **Q-3 — does the scope apply on the 3D GLOBE (photoreal)?** Option A: no — the scope is a 3D-Site (Forma) feature; the globe keeps the parcel void. Option B: yes, replacing the void (they cannot coexist, F-13). Recommendation: **A** — STR §26.1.1 makes the two views mutually exclusive, and cityweft's look is the abstract one.
- **Q-4 — the view cube.** Not built (row 24). A separate ask; recommend a per-pane control after the slider lands, not in Phase 2.
- **Q-5 — circle or rectangle by default?** Recommendation: **rectangle** (see ADR-0382 §D2 — it matches the reference and the parcel's frame, and it has no sagitta question); the toggle keeps both.

## §7 — What this audit could NOT verify without a browser

1. Whether `globe.clippingPolygons` + `inverse:true` renders cleanly with `depthTestAgainstTerrain=false` and the Forma fog/lighting (the retired build DID apply it and the founder saw terrain still visible — but that was the entity stack, not the globe; a clean re-test is owed).
2. Whether the flat-shaded slab side reads neutral under the Forma `DirectionalLight` at grazing angles (the whole point of `flat:true` is that it must, but "must" is not "does").
3. The tree-layer tile arithmetic at a 1781 m read (is it still z16 inside the 64-tile fan-out cap? `tileCountCovering` says no for Barcelona at 0.016° — trees would fall to z15 and be DELETED by the bake's `--drop-densest-as-needed`) — this is the reason F-2 cannot be closed by a constant.
4. Frame time with ~22,000 far-tier instances at 1781 m (the whole-scene budget of 14,000 is not a measured frame-time budget — `contextExtentBudget.ts` says so itself).
5. Whether Cesium's `GroundSampleBatcher` resolves the slab ring's ~200 terrain samples in ONE flight (it should — they land in the same tiles the buildings already pulled).
